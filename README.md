## Week 4 – Initial Analysis

The Week 4 stage of the HealthConnect Clinic project focused on understanding the appointment dataset, assessing data quality and defining the direction for the analysis.

The dataset contains 5,000 appointment records across 18 variables and 1,696 unique patients. The initial review identified missing values in reminder channel, distance to clinic and waiting time, as well as a date-format issue requiring validation in the booking date field.

### Business Focus

The analysis will investigate factors associated with appointment attendance and no-show behaviour, including:

- Booking lead time
- Previous no-shows
- Reminder activity
- Appointment type
- Appointment timing
- Distance to the clinic
- Waiting time
- Relevant patient characteristics

### Proposed KPIs

1. No-Show Rate
2. Appointment Attendance Rate
3. Repeat No-Show Rate
4. Reminder-Associated Attendance Rate
5. Average Booking Lead Time

### Tools

- Excel
- Power Query
- Power BI
- DAX

### Week 5 Focus

The next stage will focus on deeper exploratory analysis, KPI development, segmentation and visual analysis of appointment attendance and no-show patterns.
